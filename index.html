Auspicious Blessings of the Almighty

ॐ

In accordance with sacred Sanatana traditions, every auspicious beginning commences with the divine grace of the Gods, seeking their eternal protection and blessings for Vaishnavi & Nikhil.

॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥

वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा॥

“O Lord with curved trunk and colossal divine aura, shining with the radiance of a billion suns. Remove all obstacles from our path and bless this auspicious wedding with harmony and everlasting joy.”

॥ श्री राधा-कृष्णाभ्यां नमः ॥

मंगलम् भगवान विष्णुः मंगलम् गरुडध्वजः।
मंगलम् पुण्डरीकाक्षः मंगलाय तनो हरिः॥

“May the supreme divine embodiments of eternal unconditional love, devotion, and sacred partnership bestow their everlasting grace and prosperity upon the newly wedded couple.”
